---
title: Neutral is shutting down
description: After three years and 4,613 trees planted, Neutral is shutting down.
date: 2023-09-05
---

After three years and 4,613 trees planted, [Neutral](https://web.archive.org/web/20230530224915/https://tryneutral.com/) is shutting down.

In late 2019, bushfires spread across the east coast of Australia. By the time the [season ended](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019%E2%80%9320_Australian_bushfire_season) in March 2020, they'd burned about 24 million hectares and killed 33 people, and the smoke [killed around 445 more](https://www.canberratimes.com.au/story/6769774/bushfire-smoke-caused-445-deaths-royal-commission-hears/).

It wasn't a one-off. [CSIRO research](https://blog.csiro.au/bushfires-linked-climate-change/) links it to fire weather that's been getting worse for decades, in line with what climate change predicts.

Sydney sat under smoke and a red sky for days at a time. We called it Black Summer.

I'd always tried to make sustainable choices, but it was clear that wasn't nearly enough. So I started with my own footprint: walking instead of taking Ubers, using less electricity at home and flying less (the pandemic helped with that one).

It's close to impossible to live a carbon-neutral life, though, so I started looking at reforestation, the main way to sequester the emissions you can't avoid. The options weren't great. Most organizations only took one-off donations, most carbon calculators were inaccurate or hard to use, and the few apps in the space didn't support programs outside of Africa. I wanted to know my emissions and plant enough trees every month to cover them, ideally in Australia.

So I built Neutral. You fill in a short questionnaire about your lifestyle, and it combines your answers with U.S. EPA and other data to estimate your CO₂e emissions. Then you subscribe to a monthly reforestation plan in the country of your choice, sized to offset them. The trees were planted by organizations like [One Tree Planted](https://onetreeplanted.org), [WeForest](https://www.weforest.org) and [TIST](https://program.tist.org), and by early 2021 there were 27 programs across 17 countries to choose from.

Over three years it grew to include a carbon score you could compare with friends, a news feed, a community for questions and clean energy projects you could support alongside the trees.

Neutral was always a side project. I built it in my spare time, and almost everything subscribers paid went straight to the planting programs, which is how I wanted it. After three years, I think those subscribers are better off with organizations that do this full-time.

I've ended all active subscriptions, so nobody will be charged again. If you'd like to keep offsetting, I'd recommend [Wren](https://www.wren.co/start/neutral). It works much like Neutral, calculating your footprint and offsetting it through a monthly subscription, and they've kindly set up a page for Neutral users with a free first month. Or you can give directly to the organizations that planted Neutral's trees.
